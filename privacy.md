# Privacy policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Lebuff James is a small Discord bot that posts GIFs. This page explains the information it needs to work and how that information is used.

## What the bot uses

When someone runs a slash command, the bot receives their Discord user ID, server ID, server ownership information and the command details. This is used to check who is allowed to control the bot and carry out the requested action.

The bot saves the selected channel ID, whether automatic posting is enabled, the next scheduled posting time and recently posted GIF identifiers. This allows it to resume after a restart and avoid repeating GIFs. Technical errors may appear in hosting logs.

The bot does not intentionally read ordinary chat messages, record voice conversations, collect payment information or build user profiles. It does not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

## Where information is handled

The bot runs on Waifly and uses Discord to receive commands and post messages. GIFs are served through GIPHY media URLs. These services have their own privacy practices: [Discord](https://discord.com/privacy), [GIPHY](https://giphy.com/privacy) and [Waifly](https://waifly.com/).

Configuration is stored on the hosting server. Access is intended to be limited to the bot operator and the hosting provider as needed to provide the service. Information may also be disclosed when legally required.

## Retention and your choices

Configuration is kept while needed to operate the bot. Stopping the bot or removing it from a server may not immediately erase its saved configuration or hosting logs.

You can contact the developer to request access, correction or deletion of applicable personal information. Requests will be handled subject to applicable law and any necessary verification of ownership. Please do not post personal information or bot tokens in public GitHub issues.

## Contact

For privacy questions, data requests or suspected misuse of information, contact the developer on Discord: `26rc`.
